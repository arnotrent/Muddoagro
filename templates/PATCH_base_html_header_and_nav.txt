### PATCH 2 — animated nav underline + smoother pill hover ###
Add this to the <style> block already in base.html (anywhere after the
existing .nav-link rule is fine — it layers on top):

    .nav-link{position:relative;overflow:hidden}
    .nav-link::after{
      content:'';position:absolute;left:14px;right:14px;bottom:4px;height:2px;
      background:var(--mint-dark,var(--orange));border-radius:2px;
      transform:scaleX(0);transform-origin:left;transition:transform .25s cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1);
    }
    .nav-link:hover::after,.nav-link.active::after{transform:scaleX(1)}
    .nav-link{transition:color .2s ease, background .2s ease}
    .nav-logo{transition:transform .25s cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)}
    .nav-brand:hover .nav-logo{transform:scale(1.05) rotate(-2deg)}
